# Visual design direction

## Brand and reference

Use the supplied ZenSys logo as the source for deep navy and cyan details. The founder's Aeline reference supplies the bright sky-blue hero field, compact uppercase navigation, pill-shaped lime calls to action, and open section spacing. The blue is darkened enough to keep white hero text readable.

## Direction

Build a clear product-marketing site, not an AI-consulting clone. Use a sky-blue centered home introduction and copy/screenshot splits on product pages; use a pale blue-white canvas for supporting sections, navy for readable text, and lime only for the primary demo action. Cyan stays a quiet brand detail. Leave out fake ratings, customer counts, testimonials, AI claims, and pricing.

## Typography and layout

- Use a clean system sans-serif family across headings, navigation, body copy, and forms; distinguish roles through scale, weight, and tracking.
- Keep text measure comfortable and paragraphs short.
- Use a consistent spacing scale, pill shapes for primary actions, restrained radii for screenshots and forms, subtle borders, and low-elevation shadows.
- Use generous whitespace and strong section hierarchy instead of decorative noise.
- Keep navigation short and the Book a demo action easy to find on narrow screens.

## Product imagery

- Present screenshots inside consistent framed panels with captions that explain the workflow shown.
- Use light/dark product screenshots as supplied; choose the clearest variant for each section rather than duplicating every screen.
- Do not show a POS purchase-workflow image.
- Use GMS dashboard, active job cards, and job-card details screenshots.
- Use real shop/garage photography only when supplied and approved for public display.
- Crop responsively without cutting off relevant UI labels; provide descriptive alt text.

## Motion and accessibility

Use small transitions for menus and buttons. Avoid heavy parallax, autoplaying background video, and motion that competes with product screenshots. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing or simplifying nonessential movement. Keep keyboard focus visible, maintain readable contrast, and ensure controls have accessible names.

## Responsive behavior

Design from narrow viewports upward. Collapse navigation cleanly, stack feature sections, preserve readable screenshot crops, and keep demo actions reachable without excessive scrolling. Do not reduce the desktop site by merely shrinking it; recompose sections for touch use.
