# ZenSys Solutions website plan

## Purpose

Present ZenSys Solutions as a professional software company, explain its POS and Garage Management System (GMS), and turn interested businesses into demo requests. The launch site is English-only and mobile responsive.

## Primary audience

- Grocery shop and Food City owners evaluating POS and shop-management software.
- Garage owners and service managers evaluating garage-management software.

Initial search-market assumption: Sri Lanka, based on the `zensys.lk` domain and stated audience. Confirm before writing location-specific SEO copy.

## Main conversion

The primary call to action is **Book a demo**. Visitors submit a short form; ZenSys receives the lead at `founder@zensys.lk`. The confirmation view offers a direct WhatsApp conversation at `0755589764`.

## Product content scope

### POS

Position the product as POS plus shop management for grocery and Food City owners. Capabilities supplied by the founder include fast billing, branches, GRN and stock workflows, item management, scheduled reports, 30+ reports, sales predictions, expiry-date management and notifications, manual promotions by item/category/customer, loyalty options, shift management, receipt customization, scale connection, barcode printing, credit management, and offline sales.

The offline statement must be precise: **sales can continue offline and sync after the connection returns; purchasing and item pages require an internet connection.**

Some capabilities are configurable per customer setup. Describe them as available/configurable, not as enabled or used by every customer. Do not claim quantified results, testimonials, or customer adoption beyond evidence the founder confirms.

### GMS

Describe a workflow for garages: create customers and multiple vehicles, manage jobs, and view each vehicle’s service, spare-part, and payment history. Customer records also show sales and payment history. Spare parts can be linked to vehicle models so staff can filter for compatible parts. Additional supplied capabilities include service and shift management, assigning mechanics to jobs, mechanic work history, staff management, salary and advance tracking, billing, barcode printing, reports, and promotion messages.

Before publication, confirm that the GMS features shown on the page are live and demo-ready. Avoid implying outcomes or customer adoption without evidence.

## Trust and content rules

- Use supplied real UI screenshots and approved business/garage photographs.
- Do not publish customer, employee, vehicle-owner, or business data visible in screenshots without approval; redact it first.
- Do not present planned AI features as current product functionality.
- Do not state that all optional features are enabled for all customers.
- Keep claims specific and explain the product workflow in plain language.

## Launch exclusions

- No admin/CMS for editing products, images, or tutorials.
- No tutorial videos at launch; only the tutorials page and coming-soon content.
- No customer portal, online POS login, or product purchasing flow.
- AI features remain out of launch scope until implemented and verified.
