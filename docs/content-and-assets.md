## Copy facts and limits

- POS's primary audience: grocery shop and Food City owners.
- Five POS users use the system day to day; do not call them five customer businesses or publish a customer count until confirmed.
- POS sales can continue offline and sync after connection restoration; purchasing and item pages do not work offline.
- GMS is intended for garage owners and service managers.
- No customer quotes or quantified customer results are available for publication yet.
- Some capabilities work when configured for a customer; do not imply that every option is enabled for every customer.
- AI-related features are planned but not started; do not present them as current features.
