# SEO and discoverability plan

## Search audience and language

Launch language: English. Initial target geography is assumed to be Sri Lanka, based on the `.lk` domain and described customer base; confirm this before adding location-specific wording. Avoid repeating location phrases unnaturally.

## Page-level content

- Give Home, POS, GMS, About, Contact, Demo, Tutorials, and Privacy unique titles and descriptions. Keep the Privacy notice discoverable in the footer and sitemap.
- Use one clear page heading and descriptive section headings on each route.
- Explain capabilities and intended business users in natural language; favor useful workflow descriptions over feature-keyword lists.
- Use descriptive anchor text and internal links between products, demo, and contact pages.
- Give each screenshot alt text that explains what it shows, not keyword stuffing.
- Do not create unsupported claims, fabricated testimonials, or near-duplicate location pages.

## Technical search setup

- Render useful page copy in crawlable HTML and expose normal links to all public routes.
- Configure canonical URLs using `https://zensys.lk` after the domain is connected.
- Add `robots.txt`, XML sitemap, favicon, and social-sharing metadata/images.
- Add Organization or LocalBusiness structured data only after legal company name, address/service area, and public business details are confirmed.
- Keep the Tutorials page indexable only if it has useful explanatory content; an empty placeholder should not be indexed as a thin page until it has enough value.
- Avoid indexing form success states and any future private/admin routes.

## Launch monitoring

Verify the domain in Google Search Console, submit the sitemap, and review indexing and search queries after launch. Search visibility takes time and cannot be guaranteed by metadata alone. Keep product claims and pages updated as the software changes.
